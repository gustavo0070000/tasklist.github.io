# Design System — Gustavo & Isabella Tasklist

## Visual Direction
An illustrated, tactile **Chalkboard & Pastel Neo-Brutalist** couple productivity workspace that combines warm personality with effortless daily utility.

- **Two Crafted Themes + Auto**:
  - **Quadro Escuro (Chalkboard Dark — default)**: Charcoal `#181a20` canvas with subtle dot texture, `#21242c` elevated containers, warm cream `#fdf8e7` headlines, and crisp dark ink borders with offset shadows.
  - **Papel Claro (Warm Sketchbook Paper)**: Warm `#f7f4eb` canvas with `#ffffff` surfaces and `#0f172a` ink outlines and hard-offset shadows.
- **Signature Couple Pastel Cards**:
  - **Gustavo**: Soft Mint Teal (`#aee8e4`) with custom illustrated SVG avatar and `#0d6e72` progress bar.
  - **Isabella**: Warm Coral Peach (`#ffc4b0`) with custom illustrated SVG avatar and `#b43412` progress bar.
  - **Ambos**: Sunny Butter Yellow (`#ffec99`) with custom heart illustration and `#b91c1c` progress bar.
- **Tactile Micro-Interactions**:
  - Chunky hand-drawn checkboxes with elastic pop animation (`checkPop`), synthesized Web Audio sound effects, and pastel Canvas Confetti celebrations on 100% milestones.
  - Zero native browser `alert()`/`confirm()` dialogs — replaced everywhere with custom tactile confirmation modals and floating toast notifications featuring 1-click **Desfazer (Undo)**.
